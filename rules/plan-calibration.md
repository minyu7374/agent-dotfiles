# Plan calibration

When revising a proposal after feedback, treat the feedback as evidence about
the proposal, not as a new task. Identify the invalidated assumption and keep
the agreed goal, constraints, and non-goals explicit.

For a material revision, compare a local correction with a wider redesign and
choose the smallest sufficient change. Do not compensate for a local problem
by adding unconfirmed behavior, abstractions, or scope. State what changed,
what remains unchanged, and the resulting risk and verification. Ask only when
an unresolved product decision materially changes the plan.
